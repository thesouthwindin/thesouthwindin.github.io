Privacy Policy

Black Light Content OS · Last updated 2 November 2026

Who uses this app

Black Light Content OS is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the Black Light YouTube channel, who signs in with their own Google account.

What data it accesses

With the user's permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to read, for the signed-in user's own channel only:

  • the channel's name and its list of uploaded videos (titles, publish dates, lengths, public statistics), via the youtube.readonly scope;
  • YouTube Analytics and Reporting data for those videos (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate), via the yt-analytics.readonly scope.

Access is read-only. The app cannot upload, change or delete videos, comments or settings. It does not access any other channel's data, and it does not collect personal information about viewers.

How the data is used and stored

The data is saved as files on the channel owner's own computer and is used only to help the owner review how their videos perform. The Google sign-in token is stored on that computer and is never published.

The data is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

Cookies

The app is a command-line tool and does not use cookies. These web pages do not set cookies either.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored data. Data the app saved is otherwise kept until the owner deletes it.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
